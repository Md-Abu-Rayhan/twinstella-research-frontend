Integrity & Standards

Service Boundaries & Publication Ethics

Twinstella operates strictly under international academic integrity standards. We support authors in presenting their own research — we do not engage in dishonest academic practices.

100% Client IP & Authorship

The client/researcher retains full intellectual property and remains solely responsible for scientific claims. All code, datasets, and drafts are protected under strict NDA.

Zero Ghostwriting Policy

In accordance with COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) rules, we never sell ghostwritten papers or trade authorship. We edit, advise, and format author-provided research.

Zero Data Fabrication

We never fabricate synthetic datasets to falsify results, invent experiments, or manipulate statistical metrics. Empirical evaluations are grounded strictly in real findings.

No Predatory Guarantees

We do not guarantee journal acceptance or indexing, as final publication decisions rest exclusively with independent journal editors and peer review referees.

Transparent Compute Costs

Cloud GPU compute (A100/H100), proprietary dataset access, and software licenses are separately disclosed and scoped prior to technical experimentation.

Written Milestone Scopes

Every quote clearly defines the research question, dataset boundaries, deliverables, timeline, revisions, and milestone triggers to protect both researcher and team.

Editorial Rigor

AI/ML Editorial Redline Sample

Compare an initial author draft with Twinstella's substantive technical edit. Notice the transition to formal mathematical terminology, empirical precision, and proper IEEE statistical reporting.

Initial Author Draft

Original Methodology Excerpt

"In this study, artificial intelligence is very important and we decided to build a deep learning neural model that works better than older methods. We collected some data from 150 patients which was not very clean but we tried our best. The test results show a big improvement of 12% which proves our method is the best in the market."

Editorial Assessment: Colloquial phrasing, missing empirical justification, ambiguous preprocessing description, lack of baseline architecture details, and unsubstantiated claims ("best in the market").

Twinstella Substantive Polish

Peer-Review Ready Text

"Recent advances in clinical informatics demonstrate the efficacy of convolutional architectures in predictive diagnostics. Here, we propose an attention-gated neural network designed to mitigate gradient degradation in sparse biometric series. In a cohort of 150 stratified clinical subjects, data were normalized via min-max scaling following interquartile outlier filtration (IQR > 1.5). Empirical evaluation demonstrates a statistically significant 12.4% gain in F1-score (p < 0.01) compared to baseline Transformer models."

Editorial Enhancements: Objective scientific phrasing, concrete mathematical preprocessing description, exact statistical reporting with p-values, and publication-ready alignment with IEEE/Elsevier expectations.

Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries regarding our confidentiality protocols, technical scopes, and pricing policies.

Is my unpublished research, model code, and dataset strictly confidential?

Yes. All work is conducted under strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). Your research ideas, algorithms, raw datasets, findings, and drafts remain 100% your intellectual property. We never publish, share, or train external AI models on your materials.

Does Twinstella offer ghostwritten papers or guaranteed publication?

Strictly no. In accordance with COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) rules, we do not ghostwrite papers, falsify datasets, or sell authorship. Acceptance decisions rest solely with independent journal editors. We provide technical consultation, experiment evaluation, and manuscript polishing so that your research meets high peer-review standards.

How are cloud compute and GPU costs handled for ML experiments?

For machine learning experiment engagements, large-scale hyperparameter tuning, cloud GPU compute (e.g. AWS, Google Cloud, Lambda Labs), and paid proprietary datasets are separately disclosed and agreed in the project scope.

What are the accepted international and local payment methods?

We bill in USD for international clients via Wise, Payoneer, Wire Transfer, and International Credit/Debit Cards. For researchers in Bangladesh, we support local bank transfer, bKash, and Nagad at prevailing exchange rates.

Do you support LaTeX files as well as Microsoft Word?

Yes. We work fluently with both Microsoft Word (Track Changes) and LaTeX (.tex source files, Overleaf projects, and BibTeX libraries) for IEEE, ACM, Springer, and Elsevier submissions.
